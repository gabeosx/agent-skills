# Cycle 1

## Hypothesis

The baseline can produce attractive, functioning interfaces. All six independently executed browser suites passed. The captured cases still show opportunities where equal-weight outer scaffolding consumes room before the work: BrightCart gives a broad desktop rail to a single destination, Sensory Bench uses much of its narrow first screen before the comparison starts and Fieldmark stretches short input fields across a wide panel. The Olfacto hero has a compelling visual but depicts a bottle rather than the assortment intelligence buyers are considering. The reusable CSS also relies on a global reset and includes several failing text palettes even though individual actors can override them.

A shorter, feature-first workflow with explicit product evidence, task-shaped layout density, art direction and one browser edit pass should raise visual and responsive quality without weakening the functional baseline. The revision replaces hard-coded dimensions, default report formatting and conflicting stop rules. It keeps reusable design roles, preserves reference paths, scopes prototype styles, corrects foreground roles and adds a Tailwind v4 path.

## Comparison

Evaluate all six frozen development tasks against the recorded 1.0.0 baseline with fresh actors and blind scoring.

## Result

Accepted as the development leader. The candidate scored 88.00 versus 86.17 and won four of six blind pairs. All six tasks remained functional, with no new acceptance blocker. Brighter scope context on the dashboard, a product-evidence-led landing page, a shorter mobile comparison lead-in and clearer late-stop signals accounted for the wins. The form lost six visual points, chiefly where its type weights, dark progress rail and focused-error outlines competed. Acceptance rests on the form still scoring 84, passing the full request path and preserving a usable mobile error state. Documentation lost two points with low-confidence preference, chiefly from tight tracking and small outline links. Typography declined by 0.83 points across the six tasks and warrants a focused follow-up.
