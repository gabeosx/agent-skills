Restore prime

Refill and restart a pump safely after a routine loss of prime.

Safety notice

Do not run the pump dry.

On this page

  • Isolate the pump
  • Refill the chamber
  • Verify flow
1

Isolate the pump

Switch the pump off, apply the site lockout procedure, and verify zero energy before opening the fill port.

2

Refill the chamber

Open the fill port and add approved priming fluid until the chamber is full. Close the fill port.

3

Verify flow

Clear the work area, follow the site restart procedure, and confirm flow on the gauge.

If flow does not recover, stop the pump and notify maintenance.
