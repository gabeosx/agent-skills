Restore prime

Refill the pump and confirm flow after a routine loss of prime.

!

Safety noticeDo not run the pump dry.

On this page

  • Isolate the pump
  • Refill the chamber
  • Verify flow

  1. Isolate the pump

    Switch off the pump, apply your site’s lockout procedure, and verify zero energy before opening the fill port.

  2. Refill the chamber

    Open the fill port and add approved priming fluid until the chamber is full. Close the fill port.

  3. Verify flow

    Clear the work area, follow your site’s restart procedure, and confirm flow on the gauge.

    If flow does not recover, stop the pump and notify maintenance.
