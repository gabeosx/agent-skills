Procedures / Restore prime

Restore prime

Follow this procedure to restore pump flow after a routine loss of prime.

!

Safety notice

Do not run the pump dry.

Keep the pump isolated until the chamber is full and the fill port is closed.

On this page

  • Isolate the pump
  • Refill the chamber
  • Verify flow
1

Isolate the pump

Switch the pump off. Apply your site’s lockout procedure and verify zero energy before opening the fill port.

2

Refill the chamber

Open the fill port and add approved priming fluid until the chamber is full. Close the fill port before restarting the pump.

3

Verify flow

Clear the work area, follow your site’s restart procedure, and confirm flow on the gauge.

If flow does not recover, stop the pump and notify maintenance.
