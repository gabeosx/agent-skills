Procedures / Restore prime

Restore prime

Restore flow after a routine loss of prime by refilling the pump chamber and confirming flow on restart.

!

Do not run the pump dry.

Fill and close the chamber before restarting the pump.

On this page

  1. Isolate the pump
  2. Refill the chamber
  3. Verify flow
1

Isolate the pump

Switch the pump off. Apply your site’s lockout procedure and verify zero energy before opening the fill port.

2

Refill the chamber

Open the fill port and add approved priming fluid until the chamber is full. Close the fill port securely.

3

Verify flow

Clear the work area, follow your site’s restart procedure, and confirm flow on the gauge.

If flow does not recover, stop the pump and notify maintenance.
