## Hypotheses and decisions

| Hypothesis | Observed result | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bind membership once, then retain identities and account for each effect | Repaired ranked execution without reselecting targets from changed scores | Use for fixed ranked subsets and complete captured member sets |
| Gather all structural windows of one capture before interpreting the set | Exposed missing targets, but initially left selection on the last window and caused a wrong vote | Restore the original window before selection; distinguish capture coverage from website coverage |
| Apply collection discovery and execution accounting to every workflow | Unconditional v13 lost a public community-selection task; its seven-case sample was 4/7 versus v10's 5/7 | Have Jev route coherent repeated-effect goals; preserve the general controller for other workflows |
| Carry a membership rule through actual site pagination | A practiced six-page case passed, but a later eight-page author case produced wrong-author effects after two distinct repairs | Disable specialized multi-page member traversal by default; retain general-controller fallback and failed trials |

The caller supplies the original goal and known exact values. No caller plan, benchmark ID, hidden route or database answer enters Jev. Jev interprets collection identity, coverage, ranking, membership and effects. Code preserves exact identities, sorts model-bound numeric values, counts obligations and confines dispatch to authorized controls owned by the pending target. These remain fallible model bindings, labeled in the returned `executionContract` ledger.

Semantic collection matching accepts ordinary display prefixes such as `/f/`; it does not demand literal equality with the goal. Numeric extraction preserves comma-grouped values without treating unsupported comma-decimal values as partial integers. Structural ownership includes layout-table rows. Unknown meanings remain unknown.

## Remaining boundary

The improvement is stable execution after a justified binding. Discovering the right collection from vague site context, binding authors and other relationships across pages, and recognizing missing content remain unreliable. General-controller fallback can still attempt these workflows; it is not proof of scope or success. Handoffs expose unfinished intent and observations, and caller assistance must continue to be counted.

A future experiment should isolate relation evidence before adding more controller stages: compare judgments on exact observed item/author relationships, with balanced wrong-author counterexamples and an independently authored transfer set, before allowing effects. These results do not justify another same-model veto or requiring caller plans.
