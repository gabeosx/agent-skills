## Frozen experiment

Development and confirmation were selected before opening new outcomes. The development cohort has 16 tasks across Shopping Admin, Reddit and GitLab. Two previously inspected report tasks are additional date-development cases and do not enlarge the primary cohort. Earlier attempts remain part of their history.

Confirmation reserves 48 instances: 16 Shopping Admin and 32 GitLab, covering 21 task families and no reports. The 16 GitLab families are absent from development; the five Admin families overlap development and measure instance transfer. Prior exposure checks include actual goal-bearing traces, including large reports. Reddit lacks enough unexposed mutation instances for this confirmation set. Confirmation goals remain unavailable to implementation workers until the candidate is frozen.

Each primary arm gets one helper invocation, 60 browser actions, 180 decisions and 300 seconds, without caller intervention. The baseline is byte-preserved v17. Every candidate and executed harness has a source manifest; failed candidates and interrupted attempts are retained.

Acceptance requires all 48 planned pairs to be accounted for, at least 40 valid pairs, at least ten families and at least two sites. Strict completion requires independently established exact goal and scope, a complete helper report and no caller rescue. The candidate must reach at least 60% strict completion and improve at least 20 percentage points using the original 48-task denominator, with positive gain on every site. Both the exact paired test and the exact family-cluster sign-flip test must have p<0.025 for the first confirmation. New wrong effects or false completions block acceptance. Unknown effects do not pass an audit.

These criteria were frozen before outcomes. Later feedback-driven comparisons require fresh confirmation and the predeclared decreasing significance threshold, not reuse of an inspected set. An independently proven infrastructure failure may exclude a pair only with hash-bound no-effect and restoration evidence; an actor interruption remains an actor failure. Exclusions cannot create unmatched wins or shrink the material-gain denominator.

Private reproducibility records are under `.runs/broad-rc-20261001/`: `protocol.json`, two prospective amendments, `effective-protocol.json`, source freezes, append-only paid dispatch records and raw arm receipts. ## General changes and separate evidence

The engineering instructions and skill entrypoints follow the [official Astra skill guidance](https://developers.openai.com/blog/rethinking-skills-and-prompts-for-gpt-6-astra) and [latest-model prompting guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latest-model). They honor existing authorization, keep routing concise and move conditional mechanics into references. These changes do not assume Jev has Astra's capabilities.

Candidate v1 adds observed navigation context and Back recovery, indexed capture sections, and a return to the main capture after browser gestures. A neutral test reproduces the old capture-offset leak. That is a separate defect: the previous bulk-product trace already opened its editor at capture section zero and subsequently chose option inventories. The direct index addresses that observed navigation problem; the offset leak is not claimed as its proven cause. Independent review also tightened URL redaction and ensured Back remains available when controls span multiple candidate pages.

Candidate v2 adds a ledger of relative numeric writes, keyed to model-bound observed record identity and logical field. It retains original values, prevents duplicate arithmetic across resume and uncertain dispatch, and allows separate stably identified records or fields. A neutral reproduction shows v1 changing only the first of two records while v2 changes both from their original values. Four browser transport checks with scripted decisions confirm the expected first-only versus two-edit behavior and unchanged unrelated fields. These are mechanism checks with zero model calls, not Jev task completions. Independent adversarial review caught and fixed duplicate edits caused by a field changing its input type.

The subsequent eight-arm authored Jev browser comparison is narrower than that scripted result.
