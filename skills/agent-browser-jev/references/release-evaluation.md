No actor changes, paid retries or outcome-based substitutions occurred.

The private evidence bundle is `.runs/release-evaluation-20260930/`. Its plan, frozen sources, raw reports, checkpointed attempts, hash-bound reviews, provider meter and cleanup records remain preserved. The [persistent dashboard](http://127.0.0.1:8765/) presents the three cohorts separately.

There are **77 reward ties, one candidate regression and no wins**. In the autocomplete regression, the candidate's action reviewer calls an authorized search prefix unsupported and hands back without a gesture. The prior arm enters the prefix, selects the matching observed suggestion and submits. The scored failure remains. One paired difference does not establish a causal defect in the newly added routes.

Both arms miss all 18 instances in nine families: ascending numbers, number drawing, image-only social controls, angle bisection, circle positioning, calendar dragging, word finding, food ordering and phone lookup. Twenty-nine families pass both seeds in both arms; autocomplete passes both in the prior arm and one in the candidate. Earlier perfect samples covered fewer families and cannot be compared as total benchmark scores.

The candidate's four rewarded incomplete returns are two inbox deletions and two flight bookings. They support assessing achieved work at handoff; they do not become autonomous complete reports. Additional semantic action scope is unassessed globally. Official reward does not certify the absence of unrelated effects. All 78 pair containers were removed, with no infrastructure-incomplete comparisons.

Product discovery exhausts decisions; price editing returns after an unknown generic-control click; the page-title and notification goals hand back; tracking requests input before reaching the relevant form. The cancellation trace targets the correct order and its stored state/status become canceled. The order-item table also changes, but the harness lacks its row-level projection. The other eight unfinished arms have only navigation/search preparation and unchanged source-table digests. No contradicted completion is observed in this cohort. There is no new WebArena improvement claim.

## Independent caller: two newly evaluated templates

Tasks 501 and 538 were selected from metadata for an original-goal comparison among standalone Jev, a direct GPT-5.4 caller, and a caller with Jev. These templates were newly evaluated in this repository; this is not a model-training contamination claim. Delegation is required initially in the assisted arm. The bounded caller is an API model with UI tools, not a full Codex session.

All four caller trials stop on **spend reservation**. The assisted trials never reach caller browser recovery. Lower assisted cost reflects earlier stops, not improved efficiency. Standalone Jev requests an input value after navigation. No stock or address change is saved. Three direct-caller address attempts are rejected before dispatch because their references lack a fresh observation; the raw count of 12 attempts includes those, with nine confirmed browser commands separately reviewed.

The product-grid snapshot returns roughly half a megabyte of page text and control metadata. Its next caller request requires a conservative reservation of about $3.05, above the $0.60 trial cap. Repeated address snapshots also exhaust the available reservation before saving. These outcomes measure the frozen transport and limits; they do not prove that an independent caller cannot recover the tasks or establish a delegation advantage.

An unpaid reconstruction of the blocked requests matches their recorded reservations. Dropping duplicated control metadata still requires about $2.01. A bounded head-and-tail text sample reduces the reservation to **$0.107–$0.124**, within the cap. This is a cost-feasibility probe only: it does not validate reachability, fresh references, collection coverage or successful recovery. The next justified experiment is task-agnostic paged caller observation with explicit omissions and observed-reference checks, qualified on rendered controls before paid scoring. It should preserve these failures and use fresh instances, rather than rescore the primary matrix.
