# Procedural-writing regression review

Compare matched drafts using the same source, current evidence, task, host model,
and review assistance. Use the previous skill and an ordinary no-skill draft as
separate baselines. Keep assertions and expected outputs out of actor input.
Reserve confirmation cases before inspecting development outcomes.

Check what a reader can do:

- Does each consequential check name an observable condition, supported location,
  expected value or state, and supported remedy? A screen's existence alone does
  not establish that it displays the needed information.
- Does the normal route remain clear, with recovery tied to the actual symptom?
- Does a substantial rewrite preserve material warnings, prerequisites, quantities,
  irreversible effects, and unsupported cases? Compare meaning against both the
  original and current evidence; retire restrictions only when evidence supports it.
- Does missing evidence remain unknown rather than become a product capability,
  a successful result, or a failed result?
- Do nonprocedural controls retain their voice and requested editing scope?

Use a reviewer separate from the drafter. Give that reviewer the task and source,
all anonymized drafts, and the assertions. Record assertion evidence and usefulness,
fidelity, and naturalness scores from 1 to 5. Identify critical, substantive, and
minor issues separately. Preference ties are valid. Do not treat brevity or an
absence of particular words as proof of quality.

Accept a candidate with all task assertions and relevant exact checks passing,
no critical or substantive defect, and usefulness and fidelity at least 4/5.
Preserve failed candidates and first scores. If confirmation motivates an edit,
that set becomes development material; reserve new confirmation before tuning.

A common review stage for every arm controls assistance but does not test whether
the skill autonomously summons a reviewer. A successful documentation workflow
with research, runtime checks, and review does not isolate the skill's contribution.
Report those conditions and unknown costs or timing honestly.
