## Recommendation

Extend the shared-dispatch pilot for four weeks at $2,400 per month, within the $3,000 monthly ceiling. Defer any commitment to permanent adoption.

## Evidence

The pilot covered 18 shifts: 11 with a shared dispatcher and 7 without. Median handoff time was 7 minutes with shared dispatch and 9 minutes without it. Each group recorded one missed handoff.

These results warrant further testing, but they do not establish that shared dispatch caused faster handoffs. Assignments were not randomized, and shared-dispatch shifts handled fewer urgent jobs. No staff survey has been conducted.

## Next decision

Use the extension to make shift assignments more comparable and conduct a staff survey. After four weeks, reconsider permanent adoption using the additional handoff results and staff feedback.
