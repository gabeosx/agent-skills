I would put this winter's library funding toward repairing the inaccessible side ramp before extending evening hours. Later hours could help shift workers, and that is a good reason to consider them. But the proposal leaves us with only one staffed entrance and provides no additional security funding. I am not comfortable extending the hours without addressing how the building would be staffed during them. Last January's count recorded 14 evening visits per night. Those visits matter, but that count gives us a limited basis for committing this budget to longer hours while the ramp remains inaccessible. Repairing it would address an access problem we already know about. We can return to evening hours with a proposal that includes the staffing and security they would require. For this winter, I would fix the ramp.
