I’d like the library to spend this winter’s available budget repairing the inaccessible side ramp before extending evening hours. Late hours could help shift workers, and that is a real reason to consider them. But last January’s count recorded 14 evening visits per night, and the current proposal would staff only one entrance while funding no additional security. Those details make me reluctant to commit the money now. An open library needs a workable plan for the people using it and the staff keeping it open. Meanwhile, the side ramp already needs repair. Making that entrance accessible would address a concrete barrier to using a place we share. I’m willing to revisit longer hours with a better staffing and security plan. For this winter, I would put the money toward getting people through the door.
