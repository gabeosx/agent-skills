Basements are a bad place to lose your work.

Customers told us they were losing notes when the connection dropped underground. Today, we’re adding offline notes to Fieldnote, our shared notebook for field technicians.

You can now keep taking notes without a connection. When you’re connected again, those notes sync. Photos still need a connection, so there’s more work for us to do there.

Offline notes are included on every plan, and your existing notebooks need no migration.

If you’ve ever climbed out of a basement and tried to reconstruct what you wrote down, this update is for you. We built it because you told us what was going wrong. Thanks for that.
