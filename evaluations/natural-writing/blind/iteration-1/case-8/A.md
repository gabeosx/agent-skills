I don't think we should extend library hours this winter while the side ramp remains inaccessible. Last January's count recorded 14 evening visits per night. Those visits matter, and later hours could help shift workers who struggle to get there during the day. But the current proposal would staff only one entrance and provides no additional security funding. I am not comfortable treating those arrangements as settled simply because we want the library to be open longer. The same budget could repair the side ramp, addressing an access problem we already know exists. My priority is to make the building accessible and give staffing and security proper consideration before adding hours. I'd support putting this winter's money into the ramp, then returning to the hours proposal with a clearer plan for how evenings would operate.
