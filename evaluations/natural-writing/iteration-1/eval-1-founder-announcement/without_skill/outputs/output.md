Today, Fieldnote gets offline notes.

If you've ever finished a job in a basement and lost your notes along with your signal, this is for you. Customers told us it was happening. We built this because a shared notebook for field technicians needs to work where the work happens.

You can now write notes offline. They'll sync when your connection returns. Photos still need a connection, so keep that in mind before heading downstairs.

Offline notes are included on every plan, and your existing notebooks need no migration.

Thanks to everyone who told us where Fieldnote was falling short. Keep telling us.
