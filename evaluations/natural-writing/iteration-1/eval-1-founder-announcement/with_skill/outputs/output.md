Today we added offline notes to Fieldnote, our shared notebook for field technicians.

Customers told us they were losing notes in basements. That's a rough place to discover your notebook needs a connection. You can now write notes offline, and they'll sync once your connection returns.

Photos still need a connection. I want to be clear about that limit so you know what to expect before heading downstairs.

Offline notes are included on every plan, and your existing notebooks need no migration. You can keep using them.

Thanks to everyone who told us where Fieldnote was falling short. This release comes directly from that feedback.
