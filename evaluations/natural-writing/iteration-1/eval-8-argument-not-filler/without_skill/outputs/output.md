I don't think we should extend library hours this winter while the side ramp remains inaccessible. Last January's count recorded 14 evening visits per night. Those visits matter, and later hours could help shift workers who struggle to get there during the day. But the current proposal would staff only one entrance and provides no additional security funding. I'd want those concerns addressed before asking library staff to cover longer evenings. More immediately, the same budget could repair the side ramp. For me, making an entrance accessible takes priority over keeping the building open later: a longer schedule offers little to someone who still faces a barrier at the door. We can revisit evening hours with a clearer staffing and security plan. This winter, I'd spend the money on the ramp and make that repair a commitment with a date attached.
