# Send tonight’s Evening East manifest

1. Open **Shipments**, select **Evening East**, and click **Send manifest**. Tonight’s batch contains **24 shipments**.
2. In the send dialog, set **Destination** to **East review**, then click **Send**.
3. Open **Review desk > East review > Incoming** and check the receiving team’s copy. The row must show:
   - **Name:** Evening East
   - **Status:** Ready
   - **Shipments:** 24

A green **Request accepted** toast confirms that the request entered the queue. Use the Incoming row to confirm that the receiving team’s copy is ready.

## If the destination is paused

If the Incoming row shows **Status = Destination paused**:

1. Open **Review desk > East review > Settings**.
2. Switch **Receiving** from **Paused** to **Active**.
3. Return to **Shipments** and send **Evening East** again with **Destination = East review**. This replaces the pending copy rather than creating a duplicate.
4. Check **Incoming** again for **Evening East**, **Status = Ready**, and **Shipments = 24**.

Other failed statuses have no documented operator remedy.

## Partner archive copy

The handover also calls for a second copy in a partner’s archive. No archive address, inspection method, or recovery procedure has been supplied, so these directions cannot verify or recover that copy. Its outcome remains unconfirmed even when the East review copy is ready.
