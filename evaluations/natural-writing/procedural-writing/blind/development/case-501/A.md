Receive a sack of flour

1. Open Items > Flour > Receive existing stock.
2. Enter Quantity as **6000**. Flour is stocked in grams, so a 6 kg sack contains 6,000 stock units.
3. Enter Cost (per stock unit) as **0.012**: $72 ÷ 6,000 grams = $0.012 per gram. There is no cost-unit selector. Do not enter the sack's $72 total or leave cost blank; a blank cost is stored as zero.
4. Before selecting Post, check that Quantity is 6000 and Cost (per stock unit) is 0.012. Correct either field directly if needed. **Post cannot be edited or undone.** Select Post when both values are correct.

To check the posted receipt, open Items > Flour > History. The row shows quantity, stock unit, unit cost and receipt ID. If a posted value is wrong, give the receipt ID to the inventory lead for an audited correction.
