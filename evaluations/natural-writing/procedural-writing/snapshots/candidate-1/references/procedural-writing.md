# Procedural writing

Use this for instructions and substantial rewrites of task-oriented documents.
Judge whether the reader can act and avoid consequential mistakes, not just whether
the prose is shorter or smoother.

## Make a check executable

Give the observable condition, its actual destination, the expected state or value,
and the supported response to a mismatch. Put the check where it affects the task.
“Resolve mapping issues” leaves the reader to discover both the issue and the remedy.
If the evidence supplies them, identify the row/status to inspect and how to choose
the correct mapping before continuing.

Use the controls, units, and remedies supported by the source or current evidence.
An instruction to inspect a unit selector is wrong if there is no such control.
Explain a required calculation when the evidence supports it. Do not invent menus,
thresholds, corrective actions, or a universal prerequisite to make a vague sentence
look complete. If a consequential detail is missing, flag the gap for verification
or ask for it when necessary; do not disguise the gap as an executable procedure.

## Keep the normal path visible

Separate required setup and routine checks from recovery that applies only to a
recognizable condition. State the condition before the remedy, and make the return
to the normal path clear. A check may be universal while its corrective action is
conditional. Do not present reconnecting, recreating records, reimporting data, or
repairing mappings as actions every reader must take.

Keep material safety warnings and prerequisites beside the action they constrain.
Troubleshooting should not interrupt every successful step or hide a mandatory check.

## Check coverage independently of polish

After a substantial rewrite, compare consequential claims in the original with the
new draft and current evidence: warnings, prerequisites, scope and quantity limits,
unit meanings, irreversible effects, unsupported cases, and recovery conditions.
Check semantic coverage; an intact heading does not prove its warning survived.
Broad wording such as “history is not recreated” may omit a distinct boundary about
source lot identifiers or expiry dates.

Have a reviewer other than the drafter perform this comparison when one is available.
Give that reviewer the original, draft, and relevant current evidence, not just the
polished draft. Repair material omissions and check the affected passages again.
If no independent reviewer is available, do a distinct coverage pass and do not claim
independent review. Keep this audit out of the artifact unless the reader needs it.

The original is not infallible. Keep a warning that still applies, update one that
current evidence changes, and retire an obsolete restriction with a reason in the
review record. Do not preserve stale claims merely to match the original. Conversely,
source silence alone does not invalidate an original warning; resolve the uncertainty.

This prose review does not verify screenshots, live UI labels, links, runtime
effects, or external-service behavior. Use the relevant documentation and domain
workflow for those checks. A whole-workflow success is not evidence that the writing
skill alone caused the result; use matched inputs and comparable review conditions
when evaluating the skill.
