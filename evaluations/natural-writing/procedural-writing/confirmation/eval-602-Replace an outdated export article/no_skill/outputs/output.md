# Export monthly records from Cove Ledger

Exports use the account shown in the top-right account label. Other tabs and saved sign-ins no longer affect the export account, so you do not need to close tabs or erase your saved sign-in before exporting.

The downloaded package contains record text and attachment filenames, **but not the attachment files**. Save any attachments you need later separately.

## Start or resume an export

1. Check the account label in the top-right corner. If you are unsure which account to use, ask your lead before continuing.
2. Open **Exports > Recent** and look for an existing queued or running job for the account and month you need. If one exists, continue with that job instead of submitting another export.
3. If there is no existing job to resume, open **Records**, select the required **Month**, and click **Export**.
4. In **Exports > Recent**, open the job and check its **Account** and **Month** fields to confirm what is being exported.

## Download and save the records

1. Wait for the job’s **Status** to become **Complete**. A queued job may take a while. Do not submit another job just because the package is not yet available.
2. When the job is complete, click **Download package**.
3. Save the package in the shared folder **Monthly review**, in a subfolder named for the selected month.
4. Save the attachment files you need later separately in that same month’s subfolder. Their filenames in the package are not copies of the files.

You may close the browser while the job is queued or running; this does not stop it. Return to **Exports > Recent** later and resume from the same job.
