Migrate opening stock to Brindle

Before migration, export a backup. Applying the import cannot be undone.

1. Under Import, choose CSV. Comments are supported in the Comment column.
2. Preview the item and quantity rows.
3. On the Apply screen, confirm that the destination workspace name is the workspace you intend to migrate into. Apply only after confirming the destination and exporting the backup.

CSV import creates opening quantities, not past transactions. Those opening quantities do not retain source lot labels or expiry dates, and later receipts do not retroactively identify the opening units. Keep the source records for recalls and expiry tracking of opening stock.
