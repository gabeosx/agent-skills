# Evening handover for the community tool library

## Close the public counter

At 6:45 p.m., tell visitors that the counter closes in fifteen minutes. Ask anyone using the demonstration bench to return equipment to the counter. Do not remove equipment while someone is using it.

At 7 p.m., let visitors already at the counter finish their current transaction. New transactions must wait until the next opening. The shift lead and one other volunteer stay until all closing checks are complete.

## Reconcile returns and reservations

Keep returned tools on the marked returns table until checked. Match each item to its loan record and count loose parts against the laminated checklist attached to its case. Record missing parts and visible damage in the loan record before closing the loan.

Put any tool with a damaged cable, cracked guard or other visible safety fault in the locked quarantine cupboard. Attach a red tag describing the observed fault. Do not test or repair suspect tools during closing.

If a borrower disputes a damage or missing-part note, record their account in the loan record. Leave the charge decision to the coordinator; do not make a new charge at closing. Direct the borrower to the coordinator's contact details on their loan receipt.

Leave undocumented items on the returns table with a dated note describing what is known. Do not guess the borrower or complete an unrelated loan. Include these items in the handover log.

Check the paper pickup list against the reservation screen. Return uncollected reserved tools to the reservation shelf with their existing name cards. Do not cancel reservations. Log any mismatch for the next lead.

## Count donations and put the room in order

With the second volunteer present, count the donation box, the only cash container. Write the total on the dated donation sheet and have both volunteers initial it. Seal the cash in an envelope marked with the date and total, then put it in the locked office drawer. Record any discrepancy and inform the coordinator; no discrepancy-resolution policy is established in the notebook.

Wipe the public counter and demonstration bench with the supplies under the sink. Return checked tools to their labeled shelves and keep exits clear. Switch off bench lights and unplug demonstration equipment. Leave the refrigerator and the building's main power switch on.

## Record the handover and secure the building

Open the single handover log in the shared shift folder. Enter the date, both volunteers' names, unresolved returns, damaged or quarantined items, reservation mismatches, cash discrepancies and follow-up needed. Include tool or loan identifiers where available.

For each follow-up, name the responsible role: the next shift lead for routine checks, the coordinator for borrower disputes and cash discrepancies, or the equipment steward for quarantined tools. Mark an issue unresolved if nobody has accepted responsibility.

Send the coordinator the log link for any borrower dispute, cash discrepancy or tool safety issue. Otherwise, leave it for the next shift lead, who acknowledges it at the start of their shift.

Before leaving, the lead checks that the quarantine cupboard and office drawer are locked. The second volunteer checks the back door. Both leave through the front entrance; the lead locks it and checks the handle. If a door will not secure, stay together inside and call the building caretaker using the number on the wall by the front entrance. Do not leave the building unsecured.
