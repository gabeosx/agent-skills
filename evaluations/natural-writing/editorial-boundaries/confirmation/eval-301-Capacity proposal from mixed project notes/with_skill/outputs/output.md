# Proposal: four-week equipment-return desk pilot

Please approve lending two Operations staff members for a four-week pilot at West Office, starting 6 May. Approval by 26 April would allow IT to schedule the one-hour training session before launch.

Equipment returns currently pass through reception at three offices, then wait for someone to forward them to IT. Last month's internal count recorded 46 returns, including 18 that stayed at reception for more than two working days. Seven employees contacted reception twice to check whether their return had been recorded. These counts identify delays and repeat enquiries within the activity counted; they do not measure all returns across the company.

The pilot would run at the existing ground-floor service counter on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 10 a.m.–2 p.m. Operations would provide two staff members for those eight hours each week. Employees could continue returning equipment through reception on other days and at other offices. Reception managers have agreed to direct employees to the pilot during its opening hours, and the West Office manager has confirmed that the counter and locked cabinet are available. No new equipment or software is requested.

IT would supply the intake checklist and training. Staff would check each employee's name against the return request, record the asset tag, issue a receipt, and put the equipment in the locked cabinet. IT would collect equipment after each session and confirm receipt in the asset register by the next working day.

We would assess whether at least 90% of pilot returns reach the register by the next working day, whether any equipment remains outside the cabinet overnight, and the staff time recorded after each session. There is no staff-time baseline, so the pilot cannot establish time savings against current practice. After four weeks, Operations and IT would recommend stopping, adjusting, or extending the arrangement.
