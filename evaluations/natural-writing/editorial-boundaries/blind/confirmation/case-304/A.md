# Evening handover for the community tool library

The public counter closes at 7 p.m. The shift lead and one other volunteer stay until all closing checks are complete.

## Close the counter

At 6:45 p.m., tell visitors that the counter closes in fifteen minutes. Ask anyone using the demonstration bench to return equipment to the counter. Do not remove equipment while someone is using it.

At 7 p.m., let visitors already at the counter finish their current transaction. New transactions must wait until the next opening.

## Reconcile returns and reservations

Keep returned tools on the marked returns table until checked. Match each item to its loan record, then count loose parts against the laminated checklist attached to the case. Record missing parts and visible damage in the loan record before closing the loan.

Put any tool with a damaged cable, cracked guard, or other visible safety fault in the locked quarantine cupboard. Attach a red tag describing the observed fault. Do not test or repair suspect tools during closing.

If a borrower disputes a damage or missing-part note, record their account in the loan record. Leave the charge decision to the coordinator; do not make a new charge at closing. Direct the borrower to the coordinator's contact details on their loan receipt.

Leave undocumented items on the returns table with a dated note describing what is known. Do not guess the borrower or close an unrelated loan. Include these items in the handover log.

Check the paper pickup list against the reservation screen. Return uncollected reserved tools to the reservation shelf with their existing name cards. Do not cancel reservations. Log any mismatch between the lists for the next lead.

## Secure donations and tidy up

With the second volunteer present, count the donation box, the library's only cash container. Write the total on the dated donation sheet and have both volunteers initial it. Seal the cash in an envelope marked with the date and total, then place it in the locked office drawer. Record any cash discrepancy and inform the coordinator; no discrepancy-resolution policy is established in the notebook.

Wipe the public counter and demonstration bench with supplies from under the sink. Return checked tools to their labeled shelves and keep exits clear. Switch off the bench lights and unplug demonstration equipment. Leave the refrigerator and the building's main power switch on.

## Record and hand over outstanding work

Open the handover log in the shared shift folder. Enter the date, both volunteers' names, unresolved returns, damaged or quarantined items, reservation mismatches, cash discrepancies, and follow-up needed. Include tool or loan identifiers where available.

Name the responsible role for each follow-up: next shift lead for routine checks, coordinator for borrower disputes and cash discrepancies, equipment steward for quarantined tools. Mark an issue unresolved if nobody has accepted responsibility yet.

Send the coordinator the log link for any borrower dispute, cash discrepancy, or tool safety issue. Otherwise leave it for the next shift lead, who acknowledges the log at the start of their shift.

## Lock up

The lead checks that the quarantine cupboard and office drawer are locked; the second volunteer checks the back door. Both leave through the front entrance. The lead locks it and checks the handle.

If any door will not secure, stay together inside and call the building caretaker using the number on the wall by the front entrance. Do not leave the building unsecured.
