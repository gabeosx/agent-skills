Fieldnote now works for notes when your connection doesn't.

Today we added offline notes to our shared notebook for field technicians. Customers told us they were losing notes in basements. That's a bad place to have your notebook let you down, and it's why we built this.

You can write notes offline, and they'll sync after your connection returns. Photos still need a connection, so keep that limit in mind when you're documenting a job.

Offline notes are included on every plan. Your existing notebooks need no migration; you can keep using them.
