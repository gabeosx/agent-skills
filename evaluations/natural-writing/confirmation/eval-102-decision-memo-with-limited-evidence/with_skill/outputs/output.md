## Recommendation

Extend the shared-dispatch pilot for four weeks. The extension costs $2,400 per month, within the $3,000 monthly ceiling. This does not commit Sable Workshop to permanent adoption.

## Evidence

The pilot covered 18 shifts: 11 with a shared dispatcher and 7 without. Median handoff time was 7 minutes on shared-dispatch shifts and 9 minutes on the others. Assignments were not randomized, and shared-dispatch shifts handled fewer urgent jobs, so the difference does not establish a causal effect. Each group recorded one missed handoff. No staff survey has been conducted.

## Next decision

Use more comparable shift assignments during the extension and conduct a staff survey. After four weeks, reconsider permanent adoption using the additional handoff results and staff feedback.
